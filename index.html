HOW TO PLAY

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Combine 3 or more consecutive blocks in a straight line summing exactly **7** to clear them.

🕹️ CONTROLS

  • ◀ / ▶ : Move Left / Right
  • ▼ : Soft Drop
  • ↻ : Rotate Blocks
  • PC: A/D or Arrows, W / Space to Rotate

💥 CLEAR RULES

  • Must be **3+ consecutive blocks** in a straight vertical/horizontal line summing exactly 7.
  • **"0"** adds 0 to the sum, but counts towards the 3-block minimum requirement (e.g. `0-2-5` clears).
  • 2-block pairs like `3-4` do **NOT** clear.
  • Chain **2+ COMBOS** for score multipliers!
